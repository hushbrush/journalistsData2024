# journalistsData2024
A dataset created by putting together the data about journalists from https://cpj.org/ , https://rsf.org/en , and https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/field/government-type/

Datasets used:
Committee to Protect Journalists: (CSV) 
Reporters without Borders:(CSV) freedom of the press index
UNESCO(json/csv): Killed List
CIA

Aim:
Create a human first approach to the journalists→Putting together the people who are missing with the countries they are missing from and the indexes there

Hypothesis:
War situations lead to negatively impacted freedom of the press
Freedom of the press is impacted by the government’s form. Democracies have more freedom of the press, dictatorships have less freedom of the press.
Choked freedom of the press is indicative of negative human conditions in general?


Perspective:
Inquiring what “rules” respect the freedom of the press. 
Freedom of the press is instrumental and fundamental to democracy.
Does that mean that democracies score the best in terms of freedom of the press?

Process:
Translating from French to English
Figuring out what wasn’t important and leaving it behind: keeping an eye on what it was I had started doing this with the intention of.
Focusing on the human first approach: decision to ensure that no nesting must happen. 
Final cleaning was an interactive process because I needed to merge to know what hadn’t worked with the merge and then keep going back and putting together the countries that didn’t match.
